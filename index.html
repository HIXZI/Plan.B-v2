Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Transparent answers regarding our procurement procedures, project controls, safety benchmarks, and bidding phases.

We operate across multiple standard commercial frameworks, including Guaranteed Maximum Price (GMP), Lump Sum General Contracting, and Integrated Project Delivery (IPD). Our pre-construction engineers provide open-book accounting and line-item transparency for every milestone.
Our pre-construction and municipal compliance teams manage the entire jurisdictional approvals process. We handle environmental reviews, geotechnical approvals, structural code filings, and MEP safety sign-offs directly with regional building departments.
We maintain direct procurement agreements with major steel fabricators, concrete suppliers, and equipment vendors. For long-lead critical infrastructure, we utilize forward-purchasing contracts and secure regional staging depots to lock in material pricing before excavation starts.
Every site is governed under our ISO 4501 safety management system. Our dedicated on-site safety directors conduct daily hazard assessments, mandatory digital toolbox talks, and subcontractor compliance audits, keeping our EMR (Experience Modification Rate) well below industry thresholds.
